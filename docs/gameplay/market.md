# Realm market

The **Market** is a fixed-price gem exchange between members of the same realm.
Choose an available card from your collection, set an asking price, review it and
confirm the listing. Buyers see the real card, rarity, series, seller's public
realm name/code and exact gem price. Search listings or switch to **My listings**
to manage yours. There are no fake sales, simulated prices or real-money payments.

An active listing reserves its card: it cannot be recycled or offered in a direct
trade. Cancel your listing to release it. Prices cannot be edited; cancel and
relist to choose a new price. A buyer's confirmation pays the seller and transfers
the original card atomically. The listing is the seller's consent at that exact
price. Purchases cannot mint duplicate cards, overdraw wallets, cross realms or
award farming XP. A received character becomes known, like a normal trade.

A completed sale has the same two-party confirmation, ledger and ownership
journal as a completed direct trade. It appears in trade history and personal
archive v9, alongside the player's own listing history. Existing public realm
trade codes identify counterparties; emails and private account IDs are not
published in the market. Encrypted fresh-database recovery preserves listings,
sale journals and retry receipts and checks they reconcile.

If the result is uncertain, keep the page open and retry the same action.
Listing, buying and cancellation use durable request receipts. Two buyers racing
for a card cannot both receive it. The server enforces membership, ownership,
reservation and the current trading enablement/price cap. Turning trading off
pauses new listings/purchases; sellers can still cancel, and committed requests
can still be recovered. No fees or automatic listing expiry are implemented.

Asking prices are selected by players. The listing form suggests the selected
card's current [character value](character-values.md), based on saved source
popularity and bounded realm wishlist demand. It never changes an existing
listing's asking price. Inspect a card to see its portrait and value evidence.
Historical card IDs, rarities and transaction receipts remain unchanged.
Dated market-price charts and auctions remain outside this slice.
